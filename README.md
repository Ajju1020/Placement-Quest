# QuestHire

> A modern recruitment and job-discovery platform prototype designed to make the hiring journey simpler, faster, and more intuitive for candidates and recruiters.

## 🚀 Live Demo

**[Open QuestHire →](https://quest-hire.preview.emergentagent.com/)**

---

## 📌 Overview

**QuestHire** is a web-based recruitment platform concept focused on improving the way candidates discover opportunities and organizations connect with potential talent.

The project combines a clean, modern user experience with a structured hiring workflow, providing a foundation that can be extended into a complete production-ready recruitment ecosystem.

The application is currently available as a live preview.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Simplify the job-search experience.
- Make recruitment workflows easier to navigate.
- Present opportunities in a clear and accessible interface.
- Create a foundation for candidate and recruiter workflows.
- Provide a responsive experience across modern devices.

## 🧩 Core Concept

QuestHire is designed around two major sides of the hiring ecosystem:

### 👨‍💻 Candidates
Discover relevant opportunities, explore job information, and move through the application journey with less friction.

### 🏢 Recruiters
Provide organizations with a structured foundation for presenting opportunities and managing the hiring process.
